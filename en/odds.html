Kanji Summon

The result of a Kanji Summon is fixed in advance for each pair of kanji (the same for everyone who picks it; 870 pairs in all). It gives denju up to SR, never UR, and it never uses paid talismans.

Shiny

Every denju has a "shiny" version that differs only in its colours (the same power and skills). Whether a denju is shiny is fixed for each talisman and kanji pair: about 2.5% of all. It does not change the rarity odds.
